Privacy Policy

PlanLens • Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview

PlanLens ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to absolute consumer privacy. PlanLens is designed as a privacy-first, on-device utility. We do not operate user accounts, track personal identities, or harvest personal data.

2. Zero Invasive Permissions

PlanLens requires ZERO invasive Android permissions:

  • NO access to your SMS or messages
  • NO access to your Contacts or phone book
  • NO access to your Call Logs or Phone State
  • NO access to your GPS or Precise Location
  • NO access to your Photos, Media, or Storage

3. 100% Local On-Device Storage

All SIM validity dates, labels, and plan notes saved within the Family SIM Expiry Tracker are stored strictly in your phone's local Room database and encrypted SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server.

4. Advertising Disclosure (Google AdMob)

To keep PlanLens free and independent without paid subscriptions, we display standard banner and interstitial ads powered by Google AdMob. Google may use your device advertising ID according to Google's Privacy Policy. Your tracked SIMs, calculations, and notes are NEVER shared with advertisers.
